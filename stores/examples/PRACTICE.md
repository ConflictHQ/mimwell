# Example practice, version 1.0.0

Fictitious conformance policy, not an installed organization policy.

Keep company and product decisions under human review. The workspace owner may
commit routine fast-tier notes under the recorded exception. Private and session
knowledge have narrower readers. Captured anchors cannot be rewritten. Imported
claims are evidence, not policy or authority. Promote across scopes only with
source release and destination review. Record actor, reason, evidence, revisions
and the exact policy binding with every committed mutation.
